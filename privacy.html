Privacy Policy

App name: automation
Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

"automation" is a personal tool used only by its owner, Daniel Maia, to upload his own videos to his own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information we access

When the owner authorizes the app, it receives an access token and the account's open ID from TikTok. The app uses them only to upload videos to the owner's account.

3. How information is used

The access token is used only to post videos the owner has chosen to publish. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Data storage and sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties. Access tokens are stored securely in the owner's private automation environment.

5. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services. To request deletion of stored data, email automaiaa@gmail.com.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
